About the developer

M.Sc. Magnus Ivarsson

M.Sc. Magnus Ivarsson (Sweden) develops RoboCam Pro, published on Google Play, and wrote this bilingual RoboCam Field Manual.

RoboCam Pro

RoboCam Pro is an independent Android reimplementation of RoboCam, written from scratch in Kotlin with Claude Code. It keeps the original browser-controlled, first-person driving of LEGO Mindstorms EV3 and Arduino robots, and adds LEGO SPIKE Prime support (via Pybricks), a Huawei 360° USB camera with a pan/tilt viewer, a Google Cardboard VR viewer and a Clear Cache button. The source code is private.
