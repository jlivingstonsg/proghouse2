## Keep the notes current as we work

Magnus wants these files to record what we discuss, so the next session knows it. Update them
**during** the conversation, not only at the end — after each request, decision or open question:

- `TODO.md` — add anything still open: questions waiting for Magnus's answer, ideas he wants later,
  follow-ups. Tick off (`[x]`) or remove items once done or decided.
- `NOTES.md` — add decisions and facts he gives (wording choices, links, names, preferences) to the
  right section, and one dated line per change to the site in **History**.
- Include the TODO/NOTES edits in the same commit as the site change they belong to.
